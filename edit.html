Scene type

Visitors can nudge the view off the point; it eases back when they let go.

Boundaries

The camera stops at walls, floors and ceilings, and passes through small things like plants and chairs. Walking always stops at everything.

Catches what slips past the walls: open doors and windows, uncaptured gaps, open scenes. The edge is found from the splat; visitors who push past it meet a soft resistance and ease back in.

For reflective or broken floors that walkers fall through: they walk on a level floor at height 0, SuperSplat's ground grid, and walls and furniture still stop them. Line the floor up with the grid in SuperSplat first.
